Research branch: `bsd-localization-audit`, based on exponent-seven commit
`640e6c91b3485e1a55c99476ecb06ebd8379740b`.

Pilot:

\[
E=389.a1:\ y^2+y=x^3+x^2-2x,\qquad
K=\mathbf Q(\sqrt{-71}),\qquad p=29.
\]

This package complements the theta-coefficient implementation being developed
separately. It contains exact arithmetic, mathematical reductions, executable
certificate checks, and a primary-source hypothesis ledger. It does not change

### A certified positive central value

The checker encloses the quadratic-twist central value in

\[
\begin{split}
0.9360088537803042525901954783264223920549
&\le L(E^{(-71)},1)\\
&\le0.9360088573972690020870684844656754102100.
\end{split}
\]

It derives 6,000 Fourier coefficients from finite-field counts and Euler
recurrences. Integer and rational interval arithmetic bound the exponential,
the finite sum, and the omitted tail. The README gives the modularity and
functional-equation inputs connecting this sum to the L-value. A second
independent reviewer checked the signs, recurrences, factor 2, rounding, and
tail estimate and reran the certificate.

### Why the class-number-seven field helps

Put omega=(1+sqrt(-71))/2 and a=(2,omega). We have

\[
\mathfrak a^7=(10+\omega),\qquad
\rho\equiv322\pmod{841},\qquad
\rho^{28}\equiv1+21\cdot29\pmod{841},
\]

where rho is the ratio of the two 29-adic values of 10+omega. Its reduction
has order 28. The usual unit-lifting argument and the ring-class exact sequence
therefore give a compatible cyclic generator at every conductor 29^m. This
resolves the nontrivial coupling between the order-seven class group and the
local group; a naive C7 x C28 model already fails at the first level.

The primes (29,omega-13) and (29,omega-17) have ordinary ideal classes a^4
and a^3. Thus an unramified character psi with psi(a)=zeta_7 supplies
chi=psi^2 with chi(p13)=zeta_7, not 1. Its values lie in Q_29. This permits
the original Castella--Hsieh auxiliary setup with conductor-one characters,
subject to the remaining class-character L-value condition.

## The immediate computation to run

### 1. Obtain seven exact Gross-point values

Construct the definite quaternion/Brandt module at 389 and its rational
eigenline for the newform of E. Try the T2=-2 eigenspace first. If it has
dimension 1, modularity and Jacquet--Langlands identify that line; otherwise
use further Hecke operators. The weight-two level-389 Sturm bound is 65.

Use a specified optimal embedding of O_K and the ordered translates
1,a,...,a^6. Evaluate the **weighted pairings** with a fixed rational
eigenvector, then clear a common denominator to obtain v0,...,v6. The exact
class ordering and the geometric interface are in
[C7_AUXILIARY_PERIOD.md](logic/C7_AUXILIARY_PERIOD.md).

Feed the actual vector to:

```sh
python research/bsd-localization/logic/c7_period.py --values-json '[v0,v1,v2,v3,v4,v5,v6]'
```

The bracketed text is a placeholder: replace it with seven integer values.
The code validates the cyclotomic algebra only. The Gross-point identification,
Brandt weights, eigenpacket, and Gross period formula must also be certified.
An unequal pair then supplies the nonzero class-character factor. If the vector
is constant, that auxiliary choice does not work; do not infer nonvanishing
from a nonzero constant vector.

### 2. Compute the first useful theta coefficient at the cheapest precision

Start with modulus 29 and conductor 29^2. The full ring class group has 5,684
elements and the wild quotient has 29 elements. Use the complete cycle generated
by the contracted ideal above 2. The projection coefficient at wild index j is
the sum of the 196 full-group coefficients at indices congruent to j modulo 29.

A nonzero quadratic coefficient modulo 29 already proves its 29-adic
nonvanishing. If that test is zero, escalate to modulus 841 and conductor 29^3:
164,836 full-group elements, wild quotient 841. Zero at any finite precision
is inconclusive about exact vanishing. This adaptive choice avoids starting
with 29 times as many group elements unnecessarily.

Use `project_cycle()` from `ring_class_projection.py` for the
trivial-tame pushforward. The producer must establish its regularization,
orientation, eigenfunction, and transition compatibility. The
[theta conventions note](literature/THETA_CONVENTIONS.md) records an unresolved
dictionary between the numerical appendix and the general theta construction;
it does not assert a printing error in the paper.

The exact lower-order zeros come from interpolation and the positive theta
functional equation. They do not follow from seeing zero residues. Exact
L(E,1)=0 is available from the certified nontorsion point and the standard
analytic-rank-zero theorem, whose primary reference is in the source ledger.

### 3. Apply the existing theorem to the pilot

Once the auxiliary period and the correctly identified quadratic theta
coefficient are nonzero, the original Castella--Hsieh theorem, the localization
witness, and the two independent points give the intended pilot conclusion:
Selmer dimension 2, rational rank 2, and finite 29-primary Sha. This paragraph
is an explicit remaining dependency, not a claim that those missing
computations have been completed.

## The general research target is more precise than localization alone

Write M=E(Q) tensor Q_p and S=Sel(Q,V_p E). The exact sequence is

\[
0\longrightarrow M\longrightarrow S\longrightarrow V_p\Sha\longrightarrow0.
\]

Thus dim(S)=2 does not alone force dim(M)=2. Moreover, the generalized Kato
class lies in the localization kernel. It cannot itself be a witness that
localization is nonzero.

A useful sufficient proposition is proved in the audit: if dim(S)<=2 and a
nonzero strict-localization class lies in M, then rational rank is 2 and the
p-primary Sha is finite. Rank 1 is excluded because a non-torsion rational
point has a nonzero p-adic logarithm. The uniform breakthrough sought is a
rationality/Kummer-image theorem for such a class, or an independent
construction of two rational points, together with the required implications from analytic order to theta order. The pilot computations develop and test the relevant
arithmetic; they do not settle unrestricted rank-two BSD.

## Reproduction

From the repository root:

```sh
python research/bsd-localization/verify_all.py
```

This recomputes both arithmetic certificates and compares the logic,
cyclotomic, and projection outputs to their saved JSON. To save a fresh
execution receipt:

```sh
python research/bsd-localization/verify_all.py --receipt research/bsd-localization/verification.json
```

The receipt records exit status, elapsed time, and source/data hashes. Reading
or regenerating these files requires no Sage, Magma, Lean, GPU, or network.
The next Gross-point computation may use an available quaternion package;
its output should be exported in an independently checkable form.
