## Not asserted

No theorem above has been identified which evaluates the finite logarithm
of our specific reflected class from the already computed scalar theta
series, without an additional class/regulator comparison. This is a
statement about the results checked here, not a claim that the entire
literature contains no such theorem. The local algebra in
`FINITE_COORDINATE.md` identifies the exact additional input needed.
